## Getting Started

### What is Sound Mind?

Sound Mind is a faith-based mental wellness app that helps you take negative or overwhelming thoughts captive through a guided 6-step neuroplasticity process rooted in Scripture. Each Capture session takes about 5 minutes.

### How does the Capture Process work?

The 6-step process guides you through:

1. **Breath** — a short grounding breath.
2. **Notice** — identifying what you're thinking or feeling.
3. **Examine** — writing down the underlying lie.
4. **Surrender** — breaking agreement with it in prayer.
5. **Replace** — receiving God's truth through Scripture.
6. **Declare** — speaking the truth out loud.

After each session, you can track that thought as a **Thought Project** on the Projects tab and revisit it over a 63-day journey until the truth becomes the belief that lives loudest.

### What is a "Thought Project"?

Every Capture session creates a Thought Project — a saved entry you can come back to and "Redo" until the truth fully takes root. The Projects tab shows your active projects, captured projects, and the full history.

### What does "Captured" mean?

A thought is **Captured** when it no longer has power over you and you've replaced it with God's truth. You can mark a project as Captured from the Projects tab, and you can always reactivate it if the thought resurfaces.

### What is the felt-truth check-in?

When you revisit a Thought Project, after speaking the truth out loud we ask: *"How real does this belief feel today?"* You rate it 1–5. Over a 63-day journey (three 21-day cycles), you can watch the belief loosen its grip — visualized as a progress bar on each project card.

## The Word Tab

### What's the difference between Declarations and Listen?

- **Declarations** — written truth statements organized by theme (peace, identity, healing, etc.). Tap the heart to save your favourites and read them aloud in the full-screen Text Player.
- **Listen** — guided Christian meditations and Scripture audio playlists for moments when you want to listen rather than read.

### How do I save a declaration?

Tap the heart icon next to any declaration in The Word tab, or at Step 6 of the Capture Flow. Your favourites are accessible from the Favourites section.

### Can I add my own declarations?

Yes. In Declarations → Favourites, tap **+ Add** and type your own personal declaration. It will appear alongside the curated ones.

### What is the Gratitude Wall?

A simple, one-word prompt on the home screen lets you wire in the good. Each entry appears as a soft pill on the Gratitude tab in The Word, forming a visible record of what God has done.

## Subscription & Trial

### How does the free trial work?

Sound Mind offers a **7-day free trial** when you sign up. You'll have full access to every feature during the trial. If you don't cancel before the 7 days end, your selected subscription begins automatically.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your device's app store, not inside Sound Mind:

- **iOS:** Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Sound Mind → Cancel Subscription.
- **Android:** Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Sound Mind → Cancel.

Cancelling stops the next billing cycle. You'll keep access until the end of your current paid period.

### How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

On the paywall screen, tap **Restore Purchases**. As long as you're signed into the same App Store / Google Play account you originally purchased with, your subscription will reactivate.

### Why am I being asked to subscribe?

Sound Mind is a paid product — the 6-step Capture process, full declaration libraries, Scripture audio, and guided meditations are all part of the subscription. The 7-day free trial lets you experience everything before deciding.

## Privacy & Data

### Is my data private?

Yes. Your thoughts, journal entries, gratitude words, and check-ins are stored securely under your account and are visible only to you. We do not sell your data. Ever.

Full details are in our [Privacy Policy](./privacy.html).

### How do I export my data?

Profile → Your Data → **Export My Data**. You'll receive a JSON snapshot of everything you've entered into the App.

### How do I delete my account and data?

Profile → Your Data → **Delete Account**. This permanently deletes your Sound Mind account and erases all of your thoughts, sessions, declarations, check-ins, gratitude entries, favourites, and settings — both on your device and from our servers. After deletion you'll be signed out automatically.

This action is **immediate and irreversible**. If you want to come back later, you'll need to create a new account.

Note: deleting your account in Sound Mind does **not** cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel that separately from your device's subscription settings (see "How do I cancel my subscription?" above).

## Notifications

### How do I set a daily reminder?

Profile → Notifications → **Daily Reminder**. Pick a time that works for you. We'll send a gentle nudge to pause, breathe, and take a thought captive.

### How do I turn off email notifications?

Profile → Email Notifications. Toggle off **Weekly Progress Summary** or **Encouragement Messages** individually.

## Account

### How do I change my display name?

Profile → **Edit Profile**. Update your name and tap Save.

To change the email address you sign in with, email us at **soundmindchannel@gmail.com** and we'll help you switch.

### I forgot my password.

On the Sign In screen, tap **Forgot Password**. We'll email you a reset link.

### Can I use Face ID / Touch ID?

Yes. Right after you create your account, the App offers to enable biometric sign-in. If you skipped it then, sign out and sign back in once to see the offer again.

## Is Sound Mind Medical Advice?

**No.** Sound Mind is a faith-based wellness tool, not a substitute for professional medical or mental health care. If you are experiencing a crisis, please contact a qualified professional immediately.

- **South Africa:** SADAG Suicide Crisis Line — 0800 567 567 (24-hour helpline)
- **Other regions:** contact your local emergency services.
